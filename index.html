Who we are

Security advice you can actually put to work.

Bogolo Ba Neo Cybersecurity Solutions is a South African cybersecurity business helping small and medium-sized organisations identify cyber risks, strengthen digital environments and build practical security habits. Our approach is proportionate, clear and focused on business impact.
